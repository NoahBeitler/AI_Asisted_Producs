About Us

Who We Are

Chabad on campus, built for YU students, by people who genuinely want to know your story.

An open door, no strings attached

The Chabad Student Association at YU exists to make sure every Jewish student has a warm, welcoming home away from home — regardless of background, denomination, or how observant they are. We believe every Jew has something to offer and something to gain, and that Judaism is meant to be lived joyfully, not just learned about.

  • Ahavat Yisrael

    Love of a fellow Jew, full stop — no conditions, no litmus test.

  • Open Doors

    Every event, every class, every Shabbat table is open to any student who wants to be there.

  • Meaningful Torah

    Learning that's relevant to real life, taught at whatever pace makes sense for you.

  • Simcha

    Judaism celebrated with joy — good food, good music, good company.

🌐
A worldwide network, a local home.
Chabad is a movement within Judaism built around study, warmth, and hospitality — with centers on campuses and in communities around the world. At YU, our chapter focuses on giving students a low-pressure space to explore, ask questions, and connect with other Jewish students, alongside a full academic Jewish studies program already offered by the university.
